## 🔬 About Our Lab
The VIABLE Lab (Versatile Innovations in Affect Behavior and Learning Engineering), led by [Dr. Anthony Botelho](https://education.ufl.edu/faculty/botelho-anthony-f/), is a research laboratory at the University of Florida College of Education. Dr. Botelho, an Assistant Professor of Educational Technology, is an expert in developing and deploying technology to study student learning across educational contexts. Our lab combines theories and methodologies from education, cognitive psychology, artificial intelligence, and computer science to advance learning technologies.

### Research Vision
As a researcher, Dr. Botelho focuses on impacting learning through the blending of learning theory and quantitative methods. His work leverages ideas and methods across multiple fields including cognitive psychology, statistics, and machine learning to benefit teachers and students through the development and application of educational technology.

## 🎯 Research Areas & Expertise
Our lab focuses on developing innovative technological solutions for education through:

### Core Research Domains
- **Learning Analytics & AI in Education**  
  Developing intelligent systems for personalized learning experiences
  
- **Student Behavior & Affect Analysis**  
  Understanding and supporting student engagement and emotional states
  
- **Educational Data Mining**  
  Extracting meaningful insights from educational data to improve learning outcomes
  
### Technical Expertise
- **Adaptive Learning Technologies**  
  Building responsive systems that adapt to individual student needs
  
- **Human-in-the-Loop Systems**  
  Designing AI systems that maintain meaningful human oversight and interaction
  
- **Machine Learning Applications**  
  Applying cutting-edge ML techniques to solve educational challenges

## 🛠️ What We Build
Research systems, built to be used.

| | What it is | Where |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **VIABLE Learning Platform** | Where a teacher sets up a class by saying what they are teaching, and where learners join it. Hosted, with nothing to install. | [viablelearning.org](https://www.viablelearning.org) |
| **VIABLE Developer Platform** | Shared AI infrastructure for learning and research: agents checked against your guidance, course activities where the AI helps as far as you say, studies with consent, and an OpenAI-compatible API. | [developers.viablelab.org](https://developers.viablelab.org) |
| **VIABLE Open Kit** | Open-source tools for AI tutors that teach the way a teacher wants, on any model, MIT licensed: the VIABLE Harness goes around any AI agent, and the VIABLE Engine runs classes on your own server. | [VIABLE-Learning-Lab/viable](https://github.com/VIABLE-Learning-Lab/viable) · npm [`@viable-lab/harness`](https://www.npmjs.com/package/@viable-lab/harness), [`@viable-lab/engine`](https://www.npmjs.com/package/@viable-lab/engine) |
| **VETTING** | An independent reviewer that checks each AI reply against a teacher's rules or an answer key before a learner sees it. | [Paper](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.caeai.2026.100646) · [Guide](https://developers.viablelab.org/docs/build/vetting) · [Python](https://github.com/hichipli/vetting-python) |
| **VIABLE CLI** | Command line access to VIABLE Lab services, for people and their coding agents. | npm [`@viable-lab/cli`](https://www.npmjs.com/package/@viable-lab/cli) |
| **CHECKPOINT** | Formative assessments that help educators find and address student misconceptions in real time. | [checkpoint.viablelearning.org](https://checkpoint.viablelearning.org) |
| **LOGEN AI** | A learning objective generator built on the ARCHED framework: AI suggests and reviews objectives with Bloom's taxonomy, and educators decide. | [logen.viablelab.org](https://logen.viablelab.org) |
| **ChronoForge** | Turns a lesson into a story learners play: they investigate, decide under pressure, then compare their path with what really happened. | [forge.viablelab.org](https://forge.viablelab.org) |

More on each: [viablelab.org/products](https://www.viablelab.org/products).
